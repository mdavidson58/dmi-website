Example

A filling line loses 15% of its throughput

The same event, handled two ways.

Traditional BPA

A predefined threshold is exceeded, so the system raises an alert or opens a work order. Someone still has to work out why throughput dropped, what it is costing and what to change.

DMI-enabled BPA

  1. Detects the throughput reduction.
  2. Investigates equipment and process data.
  3. Identifies an upstream temperature variation.
  4. Determines that the variation is affecting filling performance.
  5. Calculates the financial impact.
  6. Recommends a corrective action.
  7. Simulates the proposed temperature adjustment in the digital twin and compares the predicted throughput and quality effects before the team approves it.
  8. Initiates an approved maintenance work order: in DMI, in a Teams channel, or through a Power Automate flow.
  9. Measures whether the corrective action restored performance.

Why it matters

Built for manufacturers without a full MES stack

Small and mid-sized manufacturers rarely have sophisticated MES and manufacturing analytics. DMI gives them process discovery, investigation and automation in one plant-local platform.

  • Simulate before applying: use the digital twin to evaluate proposed changes and review assumptions and predicted outcomes before changing plant operations. Explore digital twin simulation →
  • Human in the loop: DMI recommends; your team reviews the evidence and simulation, then approves before anything runs.
  • Works with the tools you have: approved actions run as DMI work orders, post to a Microsoft Teams channel, or start a Power Automate flow that can reach Planner, SharePoint or your ticketing tool. Native MES, ERP, CMMS and QMS connectors are future integrations.
  • Closed loop: every action is checked afterwards to confirm performance came back.
  • Plant-local: investigation and decisions run inside your facility.
