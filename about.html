Who we are

Industrial knowledge. Intelligent action.

CogniaWerks is a team of manufacturing, data and AI practitioners. We're building the intelligence layer that helps manufacturers understand their operations and improve them.

Our mission

Turn plant data into better decisions, where the work happens

Manufacturers have spent decades instrumenting their plants and implementing enterprise systems. The data is there, but the answers usually aren't. Our mission is to close that gap with intelligence that understands manufacturing processes, respects data ownership, and works with the systems plants already depend on.

Our approach draws on the quality principles of Shewhart and Deming. The aim is to understand variation, improve the system, and give people evidence they can act on, not to replace their judgment.

Your data, your plant

Rigor over hype

Statistically grounded analysis rather than black-box guesses.

Complement, not replace

We build on the investments plants have already made.
