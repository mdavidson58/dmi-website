Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Demo requests and contact messages. Your name, work email, company, phone number (optional), job details, industry, number of plants, areas of interest and your message.
  • Newsletter sign-ups. Your email address.
  • Emails you send us, including applications sent through the "Join us" link.

When you submit a form, your device's email program opens a pre-filled message to us, and nothing is sent until you send that email. If we later connect the forms to an online form service, that service will receive the information on our behalf.

Information collected automatically, only with your consent

If you click Accept on our cookie banner, we use Google Analytics 4 to understand how visitors use the site: pages viewed, time on page, approximate location (city or country), device and browser type, how you arrived (for example, a search engine or link), and whether you submitted a form. Google Analytics does not log or store full IP addresses. We do not use advertising or remarketing cookies, and we don't combine analytics data with information that identifies you by name.

If you click Reject or make no choice, Google Analytics is not loaded and no analytics cookies are set.

Information our service providers receive

  • Web fonts. Our pages load fonts from Google Fonts. Your browser connects to Google's servers to download them, which shares your IP address and browser details with Google.
  • Hosting. Our web host keeps standard server logs (IP address, pages requested, time and browser type) to operate and secure the site.
